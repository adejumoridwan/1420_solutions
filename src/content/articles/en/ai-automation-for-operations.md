Automation is powerful when it removes repetitive work from the daily rhythm of the business. This could mean automatically creating invoices, routing requests, or updating dashboards after a key event happens.

## The best automation opportunities

Look for tasks that are frequent, predictable, and prone to manual errors.

## Business value

When the right tasks are automated, teams spend less time on admin and more time on customer service, growth, and decision-making.
