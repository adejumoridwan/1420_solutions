ERP systems are ideal when you need standardized workflows across finance, inventory, sales, and operations. Custom software is better when you need a tailored workflow or an experience that is unique to your company.

## A practical way to decide

Use ERP for core business processes that need consistency and reporting. Use custom software when a process is specialized and cannot be handled well by a generic platform.

## The best approach is often hybrid

Many companies use ERP as the system of record and add custom tools around it for industry-specific needs.
